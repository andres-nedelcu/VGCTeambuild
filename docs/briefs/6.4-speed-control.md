## What exists now

`src/vgc/speed.py` is small and already shaped for this. Three pure functions take a
row of `mart_meta_speed` and your Speed:

- `ceiling(row)` — the fastest that threat realistically reaches; uses the Scarfed
  number only when `row.scarf_flag` is set.
- `benchmarks(row)` — the tuple of exact values, for tie detection.
- `matchup(my_speed, row) -> str` — `"You outspeed"` / `"Depends"` / `"Outsped"`,
  suffixed `" · tie"` on an exact match.

`tests/test_speed.py` drives all three with `SimpleNamespace` rows — no warehouse
needed. Extend that pattern; it is the reason this task is cheap.

`app/components/speed_view.py` renders the table and colours the verdict column via
`VERDICT_BG`, keyed on the verdict strings.

## What to build

A field-effect state, applied to the comparison, with the verdict wording following it.

**Control shape** — two controls, not one, because Tailwind and Trick Room are
orthogonal and do co-occur:

- `Tailwind`: one of *none* / *yours* / *theirs* / *both*.
- `Trick Room`: a checkbox.

Put them on the Speed tab near the "Compare against" selector, not in the sidebar —
they change one visual, unlike the meta filter which changes three.

**Arithmetic.** Tailwind doubles a side's Speed. The order of operations matters and
is not cosmetic:

    Choice Scarf is (spe * 3) // 2 — an integer floor. Tailwind is x2.
    2 * ((s*3)//2)  !=  (2s*3)//2  for odd s. They differ by 1.

Apply **Scarf first, then Tailwind** — modifiers chain in that order, each floored.
So a Scarfed threat under Tailwind is `2 * champ_scarf(spe)`. Write a test that pins
an odd-Speed case so nobody silently reorders it later. **Log this as an assumption**
in `DECISIONS.md`: it follows the standard modifier chain, and this project's
standing rule is that Speed numbers are provably correct rather than plausible. If
you can check it against the reference table that `tests/test_speed_tiers.py`
already encodes, do; if not, say in the log that it is unverified.

**Trick Room inverts the comparison, not the numbers.** Speeds stay as they are;
slower moves first. So:

- Normal: you win the turn when `my_speed > ceiling(row)`.
- Trick Room: you win the turn when `my_speed < row.spe_uninvested_neg` — their
  slowest realistic spread.

**The verdict wording must invert with it.** `"You outspeed"` is wrong under Trick
Room. Use `"You move first"` / `"Depends"` / `"They move first"`. Note that
`VERDICT_BG` in `speed_view.py` is keyed on these exact strings and the shading
matches on `.str.startswith(...)` — keep green meaning good for Andres in both modes,
which is a deliberate project-wide convention (`HANDOFF.md` §6.3).

**Ties still matter** and get more interesting: under Tailwind a tie is against the
doubled benchmark. Keep the `" · tie"` suffix working in every mode.

## Acceptance criteria

- With everything off, every verdict is **identical to today's**. Assert this.
- Your Tailwind doubles your Speed only; theirs doubles the threat's benchmarks only;
  both doubles both and (except for rounding) restores the neutral verdicts.
- Trick Room inverts every verdict and relabels all three.
- Tailwind and Trick Room together behave sanely — the doubled numbers feed the
  inverted comparison.
- The table caption states the active mode in words. A reader must never have to
  guess whether a number is Tailwind-boosted.
- `uv run pytest -q` green, new tests included, and you have opened the app and
  looked at all four modes.

## Non-goals

Everything else that touches Speed, all of which stays parked: paralysis, Icy Wind
and other drops, Speed-boosting abilities (Chlorophyll, Swift Swim, Unburden),
turn counting, and priority moves. Do not touch the meta filter, the other three
tabs, or `mart_meta_speed`'s columns. **No warehouse writes at all** — everything
here is Python over rows the mart already returns.
