# Brief 6.3 — Visual and chart polish

**Suggested model: Sonnet 5.**
**Run this AFTER `feat/sprites` merges** — sprites change several of the things
this brief would otherwise fix, and fixing them twice is waste.

**Branch:** `feat/polish`, from `main` (after sprites land).
**Read first:** `docs/briefs/OPERATING_RULES.md`, then `docs/HANDOFF.md` §6.3.

---

## The one invariant you must not break

**Green always means good for Andres.** The defensive grid and the coverage grid
deliberately invert their colour scales to preserve this, and the Speed tab's
verdict colours follow it too. It is the most important piece of visual grammar in
the tool. If a change would make green ambiguous, do something else.

Note `app/components/speed_view.py` sets explicit text colour on every light fill
(`"background-color: #bbf7d0; color: #14532d"`). That is not decoration — unreadable
text on light fills was a real bug found by looking at the app. Any new fill needs
the same treatment, and needs checking in both themes.

---

## What to do

`HANDOFF.md` §6.3 names the specific problems. In rough priority:

1. **One shared colour system.** Visuals 1 and 2 are Plotly heatmaps; 3 and 4 are
   styled dataframes. They currently do not agree. Factor the palette into one place
   rather than repeating hex codes across four components. Preserve the green rule.
2. **The Speed table has 10 columns** and scrolls horizontally on a narrow window.
   It may gain more from brief 6.4 (Tailwind/Trick Room). Note the existing decision
   that the verdict column sits *second, not last*, because a trailing column falls
   off the right edge — keep that.
3. **The threat list's progress-bar column** — decide whether it earns its width.
4. The coverage grid's angled threat labels, if sprites have not already solved them.

There is also a new caption from the meta-filter work — `"Meta: ... · N teams"` plus
a thin-sample warning — that should sit comfortably with whatever you do to the
header area. Do not remove the warning; it exists so a 152-team top-8 view cannot
be mistaken for an authoritative one.

---

## Acceptance criteria

- The four visuals look like one tool, not four.
- Green still means good, everywhere, in both light and dark themes.
- No table needs horizontal scrolling at a normal window width.
- Every existing caption still says something true. Several of them carefully state
  the limits of the underlying heuristic ("type pressure, not damage") — those are
  load-bearing, not filler. Do not trim them for looks.
- `uv run pytest -q` green, and you have opened the app and looked at **all four
  tabs**, in both themes, and taken screenshots.

## Non-goals

No new data, no new columns, no behaviour changes. If you find yourself wanting a
number the marts do not have, that is a parking-lot entry, not this task. **No
warehouse writes.**
